# Artwork and provenance

This collection contains custom interpretations of Amelia: The Last Rose of Albion,
Amumu, Hatsune Miku, Lux and Vayne from League of Legends, Rebecca from Cyberpunk: Edgerunners, and Nall.
The character identities and source designs remain those of their respective owners.

Sprites were created with image generation, then extracted and assembled into the
Codex v2 format. The most recent installed assets are included. The four chibis
received a row-wide size consistency adjustment on October 11, 2026; their gestures,
props, and look-direction order were retained. Nall retains his existing installed atlas.
Lux was created from an approved pixel chibi preview, with her staff held in her left hand
and a free right hand for waving. Her package includes all nine standard animation states
and sixteen look directions.
Vayne was created from an approved pixel chibi preview with red glasses, crimson cape,
an attached wrist crossbow and a crossbow secured on her back. Her package uses the
same nine-state, sixteen-direction format.

The Lunar collection follows the sixteen selected pixel chibi previews. It includes Alex, Luna, Mia, Jessica with curved horns, Nash, Kyle, and the selected costume variants of Dark Luna, Dragonmaster Alex, Xenobia and Ghaleon. The silver-haired Xenobia concept and blonde classic Xenobia are separate approved interpretations. Classic Luna and Althena retain their distinct reference costumes. Each new pet was generated as separate animation strips, assembled into the v2 atlas, reviewed for consistent proportions and gaze directions, and checked for transparent edges.

This repository makes no claim of official endorsement or ownership of the underlying
characters. It does not apply a blanket open-source artwork license to them.
